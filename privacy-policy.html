Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction

PinPilot is a private automation tool developed by ZiraelDesigns for managing Pinterest content for an Etsy shop.

2. Information We Access

PinPilot may access information from connected services, including Etsy and Pinterest, only when authorized by the account owner.

This information may include Etsy product listing information, Pinterest boards, Pins, and Pinterest analytics data.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through connected services is used to generate, schedule, publish, and analyze Pinterest content related to Etsy products.

4. Data Storage

PinPilot does not sell or rent personal information to third parties. Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored securely and are used only to provide the requested integrations.

5. Third-Party Services

PinPilot may connect to third-party services such as Etsy and Pinterest. Their use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

PinPilot does not share accessed account information with third parties except when necessary to operate the requested integrations or when required by law.

7. Data Deletion

The account owner may disconnect the integrations at any time. Upon request, stored application data associated with the account can be deleted.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication credentials, access tokens, and application data.

9. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact ZiraelDesigns through the contact information associated with the application.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
