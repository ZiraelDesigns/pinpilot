Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction

ZiraelDesigns Content Studio is a private content planning workspace developed by ZiraelDesigns to prepare, organize, and schedule Pinterest content related to an Etsy shop. Automatic Pinterest publishing and production analytics collection are currently not enabled.

Website: https://ziraelautomation.vesvada.store.

2. Information We Access

ZiraelDesigns Content Studio may access information from connected services, including Etsy and Pinterest, only when authorized by the account owner.

This information may include Etsy product listing information, Pinterest boards, Pins, and, if separately authorized and enabled in the future, Pinterest analytics data. Production analytics collection is currently disabled.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through connected services is used to prepare Pinterest content and SEO metadata, organize and schedule planned content, and support analytics review when authorized analytics data is available. Automatic Pinterest publishing and production analytics collection are currently disabled.

4. Data Storage

ZiraelDesigns Content Studio does not sell or rent personal information to third parties. Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored securely and are used only to provide the requested integrations.

5. Third-Party Services

ZiraelDesigns Content Studio may connect to third-party services such as Etsy and Pinterest. Their use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

ZiraelDesigns Content Studio does not share accessed account information with third parties except when necessary to operate the requested integrations or when required by law.

7. Data Deletion

The account owner may disconnect the integrations at any time. Upon request, stored application data associated with the account can be deleted.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication credentials, access tokens, and application data.

9. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact ZiraelDesigns through the contact information associated with the application.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
