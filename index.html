PinPilot

PinPilot is a Pinterest content planning workspace developed by ZiraelDesigns for organizing content related to its Etsy shop.

Plan Pinterest content

  • Prepare Pinterest SEO metadata, including Pin titles, descriptions, and keywords, for review.
  • Organize planned content with board recommendations and calendar-aware scheduling.
  • Review collected Pinterest analytics when account access and collection are available.

PinPilot currently supports local planning and preparation. Automatic Pinterest publishing and production analytics collection are not enabled; analytics depend on authorized Pinterest access and controlled activation.
